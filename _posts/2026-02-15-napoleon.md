I read this book around when Ridley Scott's *Napoleon* came out; French critics weren't the biggest fan, and indeed the movie seemed more embellished than I expected.

...

I also visited Paris soon after reading the book (by coincidence) and was surprised Napoleon was *literally everywhere*. Forget about the monuments and mausoleums, just walk around Paris or take the subway and walk through the *Gare d'Austerlitz* or *la place d'Iena*. The French really love their *Grand General* that they name streets and stations after his great victories. I also visited *l'Hotel des Invalides* which serves as a military museum centered around Napoleon's tomb.
