I am an **Algorithm Developer** at [Hudson River Trading](https://www.hudsonrivertrading.com/), and a part of HRT AI Labs. With deep learning, I write automated trading strategies that scale to every major electronic market in the world.

Previously, I completed my Master's at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), working with [Prof. Deepak Pathak](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dpathak/) in the [Robotics Institute](https://ri.cmu.edu/). My research primarily involved teaching robots to perform diverse tasks with models pretrained on egocentric human videos as a generalizable prior.

Before that, I did my Bachelor's of Science in Artificial Intelligence at CMU. I was a teaching assistant for [15-451](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15451-f21/), CMU's final algorithms class for undergrads. I also spent a summer interning at [Facebook AI](https://ai.facebook.com/) in 2021, working on a model that processes text on images in over 60 languages.
